## Implementation and compatibility

Teacher temperature is explicit resolved config. Only a changing schedule adds a resumable successful-update index; old default checkpoints retain their exact keys/architecture payload. Temperature advances once after successful optimizer/Teacher/center commit, not per microbatch, forward, validation or diagnostic. Resume restores its index. Training and medium observers use actual checkpoint temperature, not a hardcoded0.04.

Fixed prototype gain and gradient freeze are separate explicit controls; old DP and old checkpoints retain their behavior. Optimizer excludes permanently fixed g and rejects incompatible prototype-freeze state on resume.
